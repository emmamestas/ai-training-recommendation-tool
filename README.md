# AI Training Recommendation Tool — Prototype v1

## Project Overview

The purpose of this prototype is to help HR identify employee training needs and provide appropriate training recommendations. The main problems being addressed are a lack of communication, improper implementation of training, and employees feeling uncomfortable expressing concerns.

The tool is intended to support HR in identifying skill gaps and recommending training that employees and managers can understand and apply to their work environments.

## Prototype Features

1. **Job Role Selection:** HR selects an employee's job role.
2. **Skill Gap Identification:** HR identifies an area where additional training may be needed.
3. **Training Recommendation:** The tool generates a training recommendation and provides an explanation of its relevance.
4. **Human Review:** HR can accept, modify, or reject the recommendation.

## How to Use the Prototype

1. Open the prototype website.
2. Select a job role.
3. Select an identified skill gap.
4. Generate and review the training recommendation.
5. Accept, modify, or reject the recommendation.

## Data and Privacy

The prototype uses synthetic job roles, skill gaps, and predefined training recommendations. No real employee information is required.

The tool does not require users to enter personal identifiers, confidential personnel information, or credentials. HR review decisions are intended to remain within the browser rather than being stored in an external database.

The prototype should not be used to make employment decisions, including hiring, firing, promotions, or performance ratings.

## Limitations

The prototype uses predefined matching rules rather than a live AI model. Its recommendations have not been validated for real-world workplace use.

Initial usability feedback identified confusion with website navigation and the submission process. Further testing and improvements are needed before workplace deployment.

## Future Improvements

- Simplify the website layout and navigation.
- Make submission instructions clearer.
- Reduce repetitive wording in recommendation explanations.
- Expand testing across additional job roles and skill gaps.
- Continue evaluating recommendation accuracy, consistency, and fairness.
